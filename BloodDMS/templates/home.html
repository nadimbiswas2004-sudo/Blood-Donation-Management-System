Blood Donation Information

🩸 Why Donate Blood?

Blood donation can help patients who need blood during emergencies, surgery, accidents, and medical treatment.

❤️ Who Can Donate?

Eligible donors should meet the required health and age conditions. Always follow your local blood donation center's requirements.

🏥 Blood Emergency

If blood is urgently required, use the blood search and request blood options below to find suitable donors.
